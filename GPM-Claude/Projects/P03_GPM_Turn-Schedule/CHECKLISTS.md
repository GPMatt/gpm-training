# Turn checklists: preliminary split (P03, 2026-11-05)

Ownership only, worked out with Matt by interview. Field lists and allowed values come next.
AppFolio's built-in inspection form was ruled out (can't do what we need).

## 1. PMI Scope (Spencer; tenant still living there)
- Which of the 11 steps are needed + every size call (tub, flooring, drywall, paint, clean, etc.)
- Appliances: repair or replace, make + model
- Tenant damages (Spencer is the only one who records damages at the PMI). Tag (Charge Tenant);
  untagged = owner
- Pests
- Project flag for Jason
- "Other vendor" items outside the 11 steps (HVAC, water heater, big plumbing/electrical, glass, roof);
  Jason books these by hand
- Photos: every damage + trade item, appliance tags

## 2. PMI Tech Prep (Riley; Spencer fills it at cookie-cutter properties)
- Room-by-room fix list (Jason's TURN checklist 2026 is the base)
- Parts to order (feeds Jason's Ace / HD Supply email) + tools to bring
- Blinds: count, sizes, order
- Rekeying: lock type + parts
- Key-return count (keys, remotes, mailbox) on build day; missing keys get charged from this
- Items left behind
- Exterior fixture items only (garage opener, remotes, service door)
- "Not checked" marks for anything blocked while occupied (shown to Spencer at move-out)
- Photos: parts to match
- Goes to Jason, who assigns the step-7 duration (quick / basic / first-time)

## 3. Move-out / Deposit Check (Spencer alone; move-out day)
- Shows the PMI scope, the schedule and the rent-ready date for a visual check. NO per-line confirmation
- Records exceptions only: corrections to scope + anything the PMI missed
- Shows Riley's "not checked" items to look at now
- New small fixes -> added to the tech list
- Rent-ready: new date + reason only if the computed date won't hold
- Final tenant damages (Charge Tenant)
- Photos: whole empty unit (for deposit disputes)
