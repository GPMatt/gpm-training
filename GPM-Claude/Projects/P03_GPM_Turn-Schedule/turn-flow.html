GPM Turn Flow

From a confirmed move-out to rent ready. The MVP is a scheduling assistant for Spencer: he fills the PMI Scope form, the app lays out the best schedule, and Spencer books each vendor himself and checks off progress. Nothing is sent to vendors automatically yet. Dashed yellow boxes are still open.

Spencer / Jason / Riley Property manager Vendor

The flow

  1. 1

    Confirmed move-out

    AppFolio tenant tickler, now emailed daily · confirmed dates only
    • Spencer sees the confirmed move-out and starts the turn. In the MVP nothing is created automatically; the turn starts when Spencer submits the PMI Scope form (step 3).
    • PMI is due within 5 business days of the notice.
  2. 2

    Spencer books the PMI (visit 1)

    Riley walks on Mondays · cookie cutter any day

    What Spencer does, every time

    1. Email the tenant from AppFolio to set the PMI date and time. That email is also the entry notice.
    2. Create the one Unit Turn work order, picking property and unit from AppFolio's dropdown. It holds every note and photo through rent ready (billing needs them there).
    3. Not cookie cutter? Book a Monday and note Riley on the WO.
    • Cookie cutter (7 properties, list below): Spencer walks alone, 30–45 min.
    • Everything else: Riley walks with Spencer, up to 1.5 hours.
  3. 3

    PMI walk: two forms in the app

    Submitting the Scope form starts the turn and builds the schedule
    Spencer

    PMI Scope

    Which steps are needed and how big, appliances (make + model), tenant damages tagged "charge tenant", pests, project idea, other-vendor items.

    Riley

    PMI Tech Prep

    Jason's room-by-room checklist (OK / Fix / Not checked / N/A), parts to order, keys left, hours needed. Spencer fills it at cookie cutter.

    • Full paint is the default unless Spencer picks another size.
    • Photos stay on the AppFolio WO. The forms only have a "photos are on the WO" checkbox.
    OpenChecklist line items: draft sent to Spencer + Jason 11/6. Spencer: "looking forward to testing this out". The paper checklist from the 11/9 PMI (2361 #4) showed gaps; a suggested version is in the checklist mock.
  4. 4

    The app lays out the schedule; Spencer adjusts it

    Locked order · business days (no weekends, no bank holidays) · first build step the next business day after move-out
    • Every needed step gets a recommended date from the durations table. Skipped steps pull the rest forward. Only Appliances and Tech can share a day.
    • Spencer can change days, start times (10-minute picks), lengths and order. The rent-ready date stays on screen the whole time, even while he tries a "what if".
    • Rent ready = the last day of work. Green when it's at least 1 business day before move-in, red otherwise. No alerts.
    • Calendar view is colored by trade, with the unit name on every block and a unit filter.
  5. 5

    Spencer books each vendor himself

    Phone, text or email · nothing is sent by the app
    • Each step has a ready-made message (trade, address, unit, date, start time, length, scope, access). Spencer copies it and sends it himself.
    • When the vendor says yes, he clicks the bar and marks it Confirmed. The same view shows that vendor's other jobs that day across all turns.
  6. 6

    Move-out day (visit 2): Move-out / Deposit Check

    Spencer alone · keys + security deposit · exceptions only
    • The app shows the PMI scope, the schedule and the rent-ready date for a quick look. Spencer records only what's wrong or missing.
    • Anything Riley couldn't check at the PMI (blocked while occupied) shows up here.
    • New small fixes go onto the tech's list. Final tenant damages for the deposit, with whole-unit photos on the WO.
    • New scope? Spencer books that vendor ASAP and moves the later steps in the schedule.
  7. 7

    Build days, quality check, rent ready

    Spencer checks off each step · progress bar per turn
    • Visit 3 is a quality check on the tech's last day, before the cleaners. It's a checkpoint, not a step; nothing waits on it.
    • Spencer marks each step Done and the turn rent ready.
    • Mondays: 30 minutes, Spencer + Matt. Wednesdays: 30 minutes in person with Jason.
