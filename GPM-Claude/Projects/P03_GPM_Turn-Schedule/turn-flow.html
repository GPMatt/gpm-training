  2. 2

    Spencer books the PMI (visit 1)

    Spencer builds his own schedule · Riley walks on Mondays · cookie cutter any day

    What Spencer does, every time

    1. Email the tenant from AppFolio to set the PMI date and time. The email stays on the tenant's record.
    2. Create the Unit Turn work order in AppFolio for that unit. Pick the property and unit from AppFolio's dropdown; never type the address. This is the one turn WO that holds every note and photo through rent ready.
    3. Mark it as a Unit Turn so the system can tell it apart from service WOs.
    4. Set the WO's scheduled date and time to the PMI. That date is how the system knows the PMI is booked.
    5. If it's not a cookie-cutter property, book it on a Monday and assign or note Riley on the WO, so Jason knows Riley walks with Spencer.
    What the system then knows: a turn WO exists for this unit with a PMI date → the turn leaves "PMI not booked".
    Whether Riley is needed: worked out from the property itself. Not on the cookie-cutter list = Riley walk. Spencer doesn't have to decide it.
    The brief flags: confirmed move-outs with no PMI, and Riley-walk PMIs booked on a day other than Monday.
    • Cookie cutter: Spencer walks alone, 30–45 min.
    • Everything else (single-family, duplex, quad): Riley or a tech walks with Spencer, up to 1.5 hours.
    OpenHow the system reads the WO back out of AppFolio (likely a daily scheduled work order report). Matt is checking what the checklist looks like inside AppFolio. Until then, the exact fields Spencer fills may change.
    OpenWhether Spencer's AppFolio email to the tenant also counts as the entry notice.
