GPM Turn Board

Every open turn's scope as a schedule. By unit lays out each unit's steps in the locked order with date, vendor and status. By vendor shows each vendor's week across all units, so double-bookings and jobs still waiting for a date stand out. Tap any step to change it. Edits stay in this page only.

Flags: a step out of the locked order (only Appliances + Tech may share a day), a step after the rent-ready date, or a vendor over their jobs per day. Cleaner codes J, M, S, A are copied from the sheet as written.
