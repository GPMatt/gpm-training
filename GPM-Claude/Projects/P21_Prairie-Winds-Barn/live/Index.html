4Reservation agreement

You are requesting the rental of the community clubhouse. This does not include access to the Eastbrook office space, the pool, patio, grill, or any outdoor use of the parking lot or grassy space.

Fees

Half day $25 (morning: out by 3:30 PM; evening: 3:30 PM to midnight). Full day $50 (out by midnight). Holiday full day $150. A $200 security deposit is also required. The usage fee and deposit are charged to your AppFolio account.

Rentals during May and June, and rentals between and including Thanksgiving Day and New Year's Day, are subject to a $30 cancellation fee.

Terms

1) The security deposit will be returned after the event, provided the member and a clubhouse committee member have completed the checklist, no damage or loss has occurred, and there have been no infractions of the clubhouse rules.

2) The member reserving the clubhouse is responsible for payment or replacement cost for all items damaged or lost as a result of use of the property. This may result in forfeiture of the security deposit and/or additional costs to be billed. Damage bills must be paid within 15 days to avoid pool/clubhouse restrictions.

3) Lessee, its agents and invitees, hold PWHOA harmless against any claims, actions, liability and expense in connection with loss of life, personal injury, or loss of property arising out of the use of the property by the Lessee, its agents or invitees.

4) Cleaning the clubhouse is the responsibility of the lessee. The clubhouse must be cleaned and completely vacated within 1 hour of the conclusion of the event or additional hours may be charged. Smoking is not allowed within 25 feet of the building. Lessees are responsible for disposing of any cigarettes left on the ground. A clubhouse committee member will inspect the premises for violations and review the completed checklist. Not complying with all areas of the checklist will result in forfeiting all or part of the security deposit.

Eastbrook Homes reserves the right to use their sales office, located within the clubhouse, during clubhouse rental hours.

Cancellations

Cancellations 7 days or less from the event date will forfeit the rental fee; the deposit will be returned. Rentals may be rescheduled due to inclement weather one time with no penalty.

Exit checklist

Complete before vacating. These fees are deducted from the security deposit for any item left incomplete or any rule broken:

  • Cleaning (complete list available at the clubhouse): $100
  • Trash to the dumpster and cans relined (kitchen, men's & women's restrooms): $50
  • Absolutely no use of the patio or pool area: $200
  • Absolutely no tape, glitter, tacks, glue, or putty on any walls or woodwork: $100
  • Prelock the front door
  • Sign the exit checklist: $100
  • Vacate the clubhouse at the scheduled time: $50

WiFi is available in the clubhouse.

Alcohol Addendum

  • Any event with minors present must not exceed 10 minors for every adult over 21 when alcohol is served.
  • Consumption of alcohol by minors is expressly prohibited. Local Law Enforcement will be called for non-compliance.
  • No sale of alcoholic beverages is allowed at any time.
  • Admission may not be charged for any private event in the clubhouse.
  • Keg beer is allowed in designated areas with prior approval from PWHOA.
  • The Lessee is responsible for any damage occurring as a result of the event in the clubhouse and surrounding areas including the parking lot and pool areas.
  • Any spills not completely cleaned will incur an additional cleaning fee.
